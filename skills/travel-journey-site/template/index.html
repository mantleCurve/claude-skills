      05 · Air

      Breathing in smog season

      Late November into December is the dirtiest air of the year in the north. This is not a reason to cancel. It is a reason to plan the hours: mornings and nights are the worst, early afternoon is the cleanest, and a fitted N95 turns a very poor day into a workable one.
