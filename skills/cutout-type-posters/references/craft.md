# Craft notes

## Type
- Uppercase only. Heavy grotesks carry the look: Archivo (variable width 62–125: condensed for stacks, 125 for extended lines), Big Shoulders Display, Space Grotesk, Bricolage Grotesque, Schibsted Grotesk, Inter Tight, Mona Sans, Hanken Grotesk, Barlow Condensed, League Gothic, Antonio, Oswald, Dela Gothic One, Rubik Mono One, Bowlby One, Climate Crisis, Russo One, Syncopate, Michroma, Lexend Mega/Zetta, JetBrains Mono / IBM Plex Mono / Space Mono for data voices, stencils (Big Shoulders Stencil, Saira Stencil One) for crate/military looks.
- Rejected in practice: Bodoni Moda, Cormorant, Pinyon Script, Six Caps, Gloock, Instrument Serif, Abril Fatface, Anton, DM Serif Display, Italiana, Unbounded, Rozha One, Playfair Display, Fraunces, Syne. (Fraunces' black "3" also reads as a 7.)
- Two voices: one huge headline face fitted to an exact width, plus tiny tracked labels (12–15 px, letter-spacing .22–.34em) in the corners or an info row. Labels make it read "editorial".
- Fit headlines to a measured width, then align everything to one margin (48–56 px).

## Colour
- Neutral whites (#FFFFFF, #FBFBF9, #F5F2ED) or one flat bold ground (sunflower #FFD21F, cobalt, Klein blue, vermilion). One accent (red #FF2B1C) used once.
- Grade the photo bright and neutral (lift mids, slight cool in highlights, saturation ~0.88, mild unsharp). Avoid warm haze.
- B&W room + colour subject (`comp-bw-color.png`) hides clutter and makes the subject pop.

## Photo and cutout
- The cutout lets type sit behind and in front of the subject (depth sandwich). Letters behind should still read: let the subject cover at most one letter per word, or rely on repetition.
- Soft contact shadow under the feet (blurred ellipse, 16–35% black) grounds a cutout on a flat colour.
- Dark backgrounds need the eroded cutout. Bright backlight on edges in the source can't be removed without eating the subject; at phone size it reads as rim light.
- Crops: never upscale more than ~1.6×. Waist-up and face crops need the original resolution.

## Layout patterns that worked
- Stepped strips: 7 strips with gaps, the centre one wide enough for the face, outer strips shortened at the top (arch/stair silhouette).
- Stacked words behind, name in front: condensed black words fill the width behind; extended white name across the shins in front.
- Swiss: photo top, three extra-wide fitted lines, a 2 px rule, 4 small fields (NAME / AGE / SINCE / ISSUE).
- Object-as-number: the subject holds the giant number like a balloon (knot + string into the fist); a big two-line headline fills the empty corner; keep free space as margin, not as void (subject ~70% of canvas height).

## Review checklist (per design)
1. Preview at 540 px: does it read in one second? Is anything clipped, overlapping the face, or crammed against an edge?
2. Zoom on the face (sharpness, no cut), cutout edge (halo), joins (string/hand, knot/number), smallest text.
3. Copy: facts right (count days including leap days: any 3-year span containing 29 Feb is 1,096 days, not 1,095), no invented exact dates, no brand names.
4. Variety across a set: different headline font per design; watch for the same full-body pose repeating — vary crops (face, waist, detail, strips, grids).
