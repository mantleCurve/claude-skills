# 100 styles

One line each. {NAME} = the person, {N} = the age or milestone number. All type uppercase.

## Editorial & print formats
1. MAGAZINE COVER — huge masthead {NAME} behind the subject's head, cover lines down one side, issue box in a corner.
2. BROADSHEET FRONT PAGE — newspaper grid, headline "LOCAL LEGEND TURNS {N}", coarse B&W halftone photo, column rules.
3. MUSEUM EXHIBITION POSTER — "{NAME}: THE FIRST {N} YEARS", wall-label block, year range.
4. FILM POSTER — cutout hero over a darkened backdrop, condensed billing block, "IN CINEMAS …".
5. RECORD SLEEVE — square sleeve in the 4:5 with a disc sliding out, tracklist by year.
6. TRADING CARD — rounded holo border, stat bars (AGE {N} / POUT 99 / SPEED 88).
7. PASSPORT DATA PAGE — ID fields, machine-readable zone line with year/month only.
8. BOARDING PASS — ticket with stub and perforation, FROM year TO year, gate {N}, barcode.
9. TOUR GIG POSTER — "WORLD TOUR" with one stop per year.
10. FASHION LOOKBOOK — LOOK 0{N}, cutout on white, tiny garment credits.
11. VARSITY JERSEY — arched name, giant athletic numerals beside the subject, stripes.
12. SWISS POSTER WITH RED CIRCLE — strict grid, one red circle behind the subject, tabular info.
13. BAUHAUS — tiled primary-colour circles/triangles/squares, name on a black band.
14. CONSTRUCTIVIST — red/black diagonals, angled type bands, B&W subject.
15. BRUTALIST WEB — raw mono type, thick borders, visible boxes, link-blue accents.
16. TYPE REPETITION WALL — the greeting repeated in rows, outline/solid alternating, cutout over it.
17. GIANT NUMERALS — 0 and {N} fill the frame edge to edge behind the cutout.
18. YEAR-IN-REVIEW STATS — gradient card with big numbers (days, months, candles).
19. CONTACT SHEET — 35 mm contact sheet of crops with frame numbers and one grease-pencil circle.
20. TABLOID SCOOP — "EXCLUSIVE!" red/yellow/black, shouty stacked headline.
