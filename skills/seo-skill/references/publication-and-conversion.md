# Evidence-bound publication and conversion checks

Treat a service catalogue, design reference and ZIP register as planning inputs, not proof of fulfillment. Before allowing an indexable locality/service page, require reviewed territory and capability, distinct user intent, demand evidence, two distinct local proof sources, approved claims, technical/UX review and named publication approval.

Bind approvals to content version/hash, business settings and template version; re-evaluate at publish time. Preserve review history and rollback. Machine API access may create drafts but must not impersonate human approval. Password-protected staging may show a complete research catalogue without making it eligible for public indexing.

References often contain dummy phone numbers, rates, ratings, reviews and fictional integrations. Extract the layout separately from claims. Preserve authoritative business facts and flag conflicting reference data.

Test the entire lead path: invalid submissions and honeypots produce no success conversion, repeated and concurrent requests create one receipt, and the receiving system records the result. A CTA click is not a connected call or completed job. Keep personal information and free-text descriptions out of analytics. Configure consent before campaign persistence and third-party analytics.

Separate service-contact consent from optional marketing/SMS preference. Store wording version and timestamp. Preference capture does not establish an operational SMS integration. Inspect actual vendor contracts and legal scope; a corporate policy may exclude independently operated local websites.

Retain measured evidence for mobile layouts, real forms, export exclusions and release protection. Do not label implementation tests as measured field performance or live inbox delivery. Report disabled integrations and unreviewed copy explicitly.
